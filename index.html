The numbers

Abnormal return (AR) = the day's return minus the S&P's mean daily return over trading days −120 to −11. σ = AR divided by that window's standard deviation. CAR = AR summed over the window. Small samples: read these as descriptive, not proof.

Forecasts

When he sets a deadline, the model logs p(he walks it back) and an 80% range for the S&P's move on the day it resolves, before the outcome is known. Every forecast is its own git commit in data/predictions.jsonl and is never edited. Not financial advice. Small sample.

Every event links to a news report. Days the pipeline finds on its own are tagged by an LLM, checked against the hand labels first.

How reliable is the auto-tagger?

Days with |standardized AR| > 2 are scraped and tagged by an LLM. Before trusting it, it was run blind on the hand-labeled days.
