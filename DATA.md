## Outcome tags

- **Followed through**: he did what he said.
- **Walked back**: he reversed or delayed it.
- **Didn't check out**: the claim turned out false.
- **Rhetoric**: an attack or hedge with no policy behind it.
- **False report / Report / rumor**: the market moved on something he never said, or that wasn't confirmed.
- **Overruled**: a court decided it, not him.
